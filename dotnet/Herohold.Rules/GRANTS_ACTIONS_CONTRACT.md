# Native grants, resources, and attacks

These pure Rules modules return detached, source-owned projections. They never spend a resource, roll dice, heal a character, alter inventory, or write storage. The Web compositor owns adoption and the Core play transaction owns payment and undo.

`SpellGrantRules(classes, catalog, species).Derive(canonical, finalModifiers)` returns canonical grant records, camel-case-compatible casting metadata, typed source ownership, active granted names, synchronized species spell names, and warnings. `finalModifiers` contains all six final native modifiers, including active equipment once. `RawSpeciesSpellCatalog.Parse(rawRacesJson, catalog)` reads only finite structured additional-spell fields. Invalid or missing source abilities cannot authorize a cast. Configured invalid selections remain in canonical input for repair.

Grant IDs and resource IDs retain the original sorted, ASCII-escaped source-JSON SHA256 identities through `SpellGrantIdentity`. Different sources granting the same spell retain independent abilities, costs, and counters. General feats, Magic Initiate, species, active subclasses, warrior cantrips, Magical Discoveries, Wizard Mastery/Signature, Arcanum, and the explicitly reviewed feature grants are modeled. Spellcasting descriptions are never parsed at runtime.

Fixed XPHB invocation spell grants use reviewed declarations from `data/raw/5etools/optionalfeatures.json`. Their own invocation casts use no slot, at their base spell level; Gift of the Depths instead has one free cast per Long Rest. Self-only, light requirements, familiar configuration, and Fiendish Vigor's maximum temporary HP remain explicit manual conditions. They are not silently simulated. Pact of the Tome adds required `class_options.pact_tome_cantrips` (three) and `pact_tome_rituals` (two level-one rituals). These spells are prepared Warlock spells using Charisma; leveled rituals may also use slots. Removing the parent removes active grants while preserving the saved choices for repair/reselection.

`ClassChoiceContext.PreparedSpellSources` separates existing prepared spell ownership. The Tome definitions exclude spells prepared by another source and retain their own selections. Both Tome keys are owned by `ClassChoiceRules`; creation and level-up translate their generic descriptors. Other invocation instance-bound choices, such as repeated Agonizing Blast targets or an invocation-selected Origin feat, require their own explicit configuration and are not inferred from descriptions.

`ClassResourceRules(classes).Derive(canonical, build, activeFeatures)` returns the fresh derived-resource cache and stable counter metadata. Capacity formulas use final native modifiers and trusted class thresholds. Short-Rest recovery amounts are explicit, unknown recovery remains manual, and resource IDs match the existing play contract. The caller preserves current spent counts while applying capacity changes.

`AttackActionRules(weapons).Derive(canonical, build, activeFeatures, resources, inventory)` returns equipped weapon actions and eligible Monk unarmed actions. Native final modifiers, proficiency, Archery, explicit versatile modes, Dueling, and weapon mastery references are included. Carried items do not activate attacks or bonuses. Monk benefits require no armor/shield and only Simple Melee or Light Martial Melee weapons; the Martial Arts die replaces a weapon die only when its average is greater. Weapon Master feat choices contribute mastery references without consuming class selections. Mastery effects remain manual. Fixed-damage weapons unsupported by Core's dice schema produce a clear warning instead of fabricated dice.

Reviewed 2024 corrections include all ten Fighting Style feats for Paladins/Rangers, Barbarian melee-only mastery, and Monk melee-only Martial Arts eligibility. Primary reference: https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/character-classes . Raw XPHB item/feat/optional-feature records supply the structured weapon facts and reviewed Dueling/invocation wording. No numerical policy is extracted from prose at runtime.
